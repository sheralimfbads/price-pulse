Data Engineering Standards & Design Assumptions

Key guarantees enforced throughout the data lifecycle

Source Integrity & Leading Zeros

Product codes are identifiers, not numbers. Leading zeros such as 001, 0004, 0050, and 0070 are strictly preserved in string format across extraction, staging, analytics, and API models.

Zero-Price Audit Assumption

Source observations with zero prices are preserved in source_price_uzs_per_kg for audit traceability, marked as zero_marked_unavailable, and mapped to NULL in the analytical layer. No synthetic prices are imputed.

Exact Calendar Arithmetic

Year-over-year and month-over-month comparisons join on exact calendar boundaries (e.g. month_start - INTERVAL '1 year'). Missing intermediate months never produce misleading row-lag calculations.

Deterministic Historical Replay

Past snapshots can be re-executed through Airflow or CLI in isolated replay directories with cryptographic hash validation, ensuring full historical reproducibility without modifying live production analytics.
